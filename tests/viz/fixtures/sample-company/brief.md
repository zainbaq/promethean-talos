# Brief: focus (Talos v0.2 UI test)

**From:** ceo (CEO) · **Date:** 2026-10-28

A small focus timer that runs in the browser. Two screens:

1. **Timer:** a large countdown (default 25 minutes), start, pause and reset, and the current session label (focus or break).
2. **Settings:** focus length, break length and a sound on/off switch, saved in the browser so they survive a reload.

It must look deliberate and calm, work well on a phone, support dark mode, and be usable with a keyboard. No accounts, no backend, no analytics.

Scope is deliberately two backlog items, one per screen, in one cycle. Small project, core and design teams only. This project exists to test the design team and the design gate, so keep every document short.
