## Description

Why P2: the record is the company, and the two documents engineers build from disagree on an accessibility behaviour that charter criterion 6 covers. Architect note on ITEM-0002 (EVT-20261028173027-fac6): ADR-0002 says an invalid value makes a separate inline error paragraph with role=alert visible; SPEC-0002 (and the shipped app/settings.html) instead swap the field help text, which is aria-live=polite, for the rule sentence. A screen-reader user hears these differently (interrupting alert vs polite announcement). Scope: a record decision by the Chief Architect, either amending or superseding ADR-0002 to match what shipped, or amending SPEC-0002 and filing a product item to change the app. Not an engineering item on its own; if the product must change, a follow-up item is created from this one. Head of Product proposes the default: keep what shipped (polite help-text swap) and amend the ADR, since the shipped behaviour passed axe and code review.
